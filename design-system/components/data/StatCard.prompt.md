KPI tile. Four across on desktop, two across on mobile; only the first gets `glow`.

```jsx
<StatCard glow icon="wallet" value="1174" label="Total amount of orders" />
<StatCard icon="banknote" value="$8,126,420" label="Total money paid" />
```

Source figures are unformatted counts and `$`-prefixed comma-grouped money; captions are sentence case.
